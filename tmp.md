Product Polish / Retention
Book notes highlights: per-book quote/highlight entries with date and optional page number.
Reminders: optional email/push reminders for books in reading not updated in X days.
Recommendations: “similar to your finished books” (could start simple rule-based).
Goals dashboard: yearly reading goal, books/month target, completion charts.
Activity feed: “you finished X”, “started Y”, “added Z”.


Social (Optional)
Shareable public profile (opt-in): reading stats and recent books.
Friends/following: see what others are reading.
Book clubs / shared shelves: collaborative lists.

Admin / Self-Hosted Features
Audit logs: who changed signups/users/settings.
Role expansion: moderator/editor roles.
System health page: DB connectivity, migrations status, queue jobs.
Backup helper: one-click export/restore instructions for self-hosted admins.
